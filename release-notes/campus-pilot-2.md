## What's new

- **Profile → Campus life**: 12 more colleges, including Ohio State, Georgia, Penn State, UNC, Duke, Wake Forest, and
  SMU, now show facts read from their own pages: their fraternity and sorority councils and chapter counts, whether
  they have an LGBTQ+ center (and when one closed), and policies such as nondiscrimination and chosen names on
  records. Each fact links to the page it came from and the date we checked it.
- **Greek life**: a council whose chapters aren't on the national lists we read now says so, instead of looking empty.

## Behind the scenes

- This was the second test run of reading colleges' own pages with AI models, on 25 new colleges, at half the cost
  per college of the first. We checked its answers against facts gathered by hand.
- We show only kinds of facts that were right about 95% of the time. Faith offices and community size estimates
  slipped below that bar this round, so they're hidden until a better run; fraternity membership, housing, and
  recruitment timing were right but there still aren't enough of them to be sure.
