## What's new

- **Profile → Campus life**: a new "Athletics & programs" card. It shows the NCAA division (FBS, FCS, Division II or III)
  or NAIA, the conference (and football's, when different), and recent moves such as "Moved from the Pacific-12
  Conference to the Big Ten Conference". It also lists ROTC branches, study abroad, undergraduate research programs, the
  academic calendar, student services, and the share of undergrads registered with disability services.
- **Profile → Admissions**: whether the college grants credit for AP exams.
- **Explore → Sports & programs**: filter by division, football, ROTC branch, undergraduate research, and study abroad.
  Tap a conference on any profile to see every college in it.
- **Compare**: rows for athletics, conference, ROTC, study abroad, undergraduate research, calendar, and AP credit.
- **Glossary**: NCAA division, athletic conference, ROTC, study abroad, undergraduate research, academic calendar, AP
  credit, and disability services.

## Behind the scenes

- From the federal survey of what colleges offer (Institutional Characteristics), for 2025–26. No existing value changed.
- A college that doesn't tick a box on the survey may still offer the program, so the site says "Not listed" rather
  than "No".
- Conference moves come from colleges' yearly answers back to 2014–15. The federal survey records conferences but not
  divisions, so the site keeps a reviewed list of which conference is in which division, checked against each
  conference's members, and updates it every year for realignment.
