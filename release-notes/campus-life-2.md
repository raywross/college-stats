## What's new

- **Profile → Campus life → Religious life**: which faith communities have a group on campus (Chabad, Catholic
  FOCUS, Reformed University Fellowship, The Navigators, Secular Student Alliance chapters), grouped by tradition.
  Each comes from the organization's own list, credited and dated in its ⓘ. Members of the Council for Christian
  Colleges & Universities now count as "Faith-centered".
- **Profile → Campus life → Greek life**: which fraternity and sorority councils have chapters on campus (Panhellenic,
  Interfraternity, historically Black, Latino, Asian, multicultural), from each organization's chapter list. Where we
  read the college's own fraternity and sorority office, its chapter counts by council too.
- **Profile → Campus life → LGBTQ+ life**: an LGBTQ+ center or office, and a policy checklist: gender-inclusive
  housing, chosen names on records, nondiscrimination covering gender identity, and more. Each item says who lists it
  (the Trans Policy Clearinghouse or the college's own page) and when. A list never turns into a "No": if no one
  lists a policy, nothing is shown. Where a religious college's conduct code restricts same-sex relationships or
  gender expression, we quote it.
- **State laws**: public colleges in Alabama, Florida, Idaho, Iowa, Texas, and Utah show a line on the state law
  that limits identity-based offices or programs, linked to the statute.
- **Explore**: filter by colleges with a Jewish, Catholic, or other faith community; with historically Black, Latino,
  Asian, or multicultural Greek chapters; with an LGBTQ+ center, gender-inclusive housing, or a nondiscrimination
  policy covering gender identity.
- **Compare**: rows for Greek councils and the LGBTQ+ policy checklist.

## Behind the scenes

- About 30 national lists were read politely (following each site's robots.txt, identifying ourselves, never
  getting around a block) and matched to colleges by name and state, with hard cases checked by hand. Sites that
  block automated reading are recorded, not worked around.
- A pilot read 25 colleges' own pages with AI models, checked every quote against its page, and had a second model
  re-check every "No" and every conduct-code finding. Scored against a hand-checked answer key, only fact types that
  were right about 95% of the time are shown; the rest wait for a better run.
- Next: improve the per-college reading before running it on every college, read more organizations' lists, and ask
  organizations such as Hillel for their data directly. See the [roadmap](/roadmap).
