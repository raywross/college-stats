## What's planned

- **[Religious life](../specs/religious-life.md)**: religious affiliation, how central faith is to campus life, and
  the faith communities on campus.
- **[Greek life](../specs/greek-life.md)**: fraternity and sorority participation, councils, housing, and recruitment.

Each spec works through one college in detail (UT Austin), ranks sources by how much to trust them, and plans a crawl
of all 1,893 colleges, the data model, the design, and the phases.

## What the research found

- Three sources give three different numbers for UT Austin's Jewish student population (2,750, 4,000+, and about
  5,000), so the site will need to show where each figure comes from.
- UT's Common Data Set Greek-life percentages match its own chapter reports (7,191 members in spring 2026).
- Reading national organizations' directories, one crawl each, replaces most of the 10–20 lookups each college would
  otherwise need.
