## What's planned

**[National trends](../specs/national-trends.md)** looks at patterns across colleges rather than at one college:

- **Each study** asks one question and shows the national picture, the same four breakdowns every time (region, public
  or private, size, selectivity), a takeaway, a method note, and links into Explore.
- **Rules**: compare the same colleges then and now; say whether a figure counts colleges or students; at least 30
  colleges per group; describe patterns, not causes.
- **Where it appears**: a new Trends page with a card per study, a page per study, and links from college charts.

## A first look at Study 1: men and women in admissions

Across 912 colleges with both rates and 1,000+ applicants in fall 2004 and fall 2024:

- Colleges admitting men at a notably higher rate fell from 13% to 6%.
- The shift is concentrated in the Northeast (women admitted at a higher rate: 41% → 56% of colleges) and among
  moderately selective colleges (38% → 59%).
- The West moved the other way (54% → 39%).
- The most selective colleges show almost no gap.
