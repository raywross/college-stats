## What's new

- **Profile → Campus life**: more colleges now show facts read from their own pages, including Florida State, Auburn,
  LSU, Clemson, Iowa, Virginia Tech, UC Berkeley, Minnesota, Michigan State, TCU, Villanova, and Yale: their fraternity
  and sorority councils and chapter counts, and policies such as chosen names on records and gender-inclusive housing.
  Each fact links to the page it came from and the date we checked it.
- **Fewer, surer facts**: some colleges no longer show an LGBTQ+ center, a nondiscrimination answer, or council
  membership read from their own pages. Our checks found these kinds of facts were wrong too often, for example a
  short notice read as the whole policy. Where a national organization lists the center or policy, the profile still
  shows it, credited.

## Behind the scenes

- The third test run of reading colleges' own pages cost about a quarter as much per college as the first, and now
  reads fraternity and sorority reports linked from each college's office.
- Every run is checked against facts gathered by hand. Only kinds of facts that are right about 95% of the time
  appear on the site.
