## What's new

- **[Roadmap → Later](/roadmap)**: a plan for showing facts from every college's own fraternity and sorority, faith
  life, and LGBTQ+ pages, not just the 75 we've tested. It lists which kinds of facts were reliable in our tests and
  which weren't (for example, a short notice mistaken for a college's full nondiscrimination policy), what we'll
  change, and how we'll check it before running everywhere.

## Behind the scenes

- Three test runs, each checked against facts gathered by hand, cut the cost from $0.61 to $0.16 a college. A full
  run would be about $300, and would publish only the kinds of facts that pass our accuracy bar.
