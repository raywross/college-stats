# Naming study: a new name and .com for the site

> Working notes, not a work item. Started 3 October 2026. The site is currently "Quad" (`SITE_NAME` in
> `lib/brand.ts`, tagline "College data, decoded"). Nothing has been decided or registered yet.
> Visual proposals with logo mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/SGn4hiedEEUW5EC8JtJwSX (private, round two).

## Brief
- **Target market:** high school students choosing where to apply, and their parents.
- **Must:** be a .com, short and easy to remember, spellable after hearing it once, comfortable for a
  16-year-old to type and a parent to recommend.
- **Must not:** promise admission or odds. The site classifies by published rules and never gives a
  probability, so "odds", "chances" and "predict" were excluded.
- **Direction after round one (Ray):** "campus" over "college" (homey, "where am I going"); a play on the
  quad (campus quad, college quad); a navigator or analyzer theme (like campusnav). Ray's wife likes
  "stats for u". Round one's "plot" idea was not liked.

## Conflicts found
- **Quad Education Group** (quadeducationgroup.com) is an established college-admissions consultancy selling
  to the same families: school-list building, essays, test prep, offices in NYC, Boston, San Jose. A data
  site called just "Quad" would be confused with it. A two-word quad name is more distinct, but any quad
  name needs a USPTO search against their registrations before buying.
- **College Navigator** is the U.S. Department of Education's (NCES) own college data tool, and the site is
  built on its data. A name containing "navigator" will read as a copy. Wayfinding names here use
  "bearings", "heading", "wayfinder" instead.
- **Campus Quad** (campusquad.co) was a 2012 San Carlos startup selling a campus-events app to universities;
  appears dormant since about 2015. campusquad.com is registered anyway.
- **STATforU** is a University of Mons (Belgium) statistics training platform; **Stats4U** is a free stats
  package. Low risk for statsforu.com, but both exist.
- **CampusNav** is used by several university wayfinding apps (GeoTEd at Southwest Virginia CC, a Columbia
  College Chicago prototype, TU Dresden's Campus Navigator). campusnav.com is registered.

## Method
- About 575 names were queried at Verisign's RDAP service, the authoritative .com registry:
  `https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com` returns 404 for unregistered names and 200 for
  registered ones. Controls: google.com returned 200, a random string returned 404.
- .com has no registry premium tier, so a free name costs a registrar's standard fee (about $10–15/yr).
  Availability changes by the hour; register the same day as the decision.
- Web searches for each finalist found no company or product using it. That is not a trademark clearance.

## Before registering (checklist)
1. USPTO trademark search for the pick in Class 41 (education) and Class 42 (software); for a quad name,
   read Quad Education Group's registrations specifically.
2. Check the handle on Instagram and TikTok (students), X and LinkedIn (parents, counselors).
3. Register at a cost-price registrar (Cloudflare, Porkbun) with WHOIS privacy and registrar lock; buy the
   companion domains at the same time.
4. Say the name on the phone to someone who has not seen it written and ask them to type it.

## When a name is chosen
- `SITE_NAME` and tagline in `lib/brand.ts`; logo in `components/layout/Logo.tsx` (the quad marks reuse its
  four-quadrant geometry and the four brand colors, so a quad name is a wordmark change, not a redesign)
- `specs/design-system.md` Brand section; metadata titles; any "Quad" in copy and release notes
- Vercel domain, Supabase redirect URLs, and the repo name if desired
