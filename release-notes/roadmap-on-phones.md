## What's new

The phone redesign hides the footer's links and moves those pages into the tab bar's **More** menu. The Roadmap had
only been added to the footer, so phones couldn't reach it.

More now lists **Roadmap: What's coming next** after Glossary and Data, and the More tab is highlighted while you're
on a roadmap page.
