## What's new

The **Test-optional went mainstream** study used to end at the newest federal data, fall 2024, when 5% of colleges
required the SAT or ACT. It now adds a section, **This cycle, on the Common App grid**, from Common App's own
Requirements Grid for the 2026-27 cycle:

- **Four shares** for the thousand member colleges with a grid row: require a test (always, or a choice of tests),
  required for some applicants, test-optional, and test-blind, each beside the same colleges' federal answer in fall
  2024 so the two can be compared where they overlap.
- **Who requires a test again:** the colleges that were test-optional or test-blind by the federal count in fall 2024
  and tell Common App they always require one this cycle, named and linked, and the few that went the other way.
- **A caveat where it matters:** the grid's "required for some" has no federal counterpart, and most of those colleges
  read "considered but not required" in federal data, so part of the gap between the two counts is a finer instrument
  rather than a change of policy. The named list is the clearest signal.

The study's headline stays on the federal data, so it still agrees with the home page's "Test-optional went
mainstream" fact; the new section is a look ahead for Common App members, cited to the grid's cycle and update date.
