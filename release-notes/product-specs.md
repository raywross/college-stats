## What's planned

The [roadmap](/roadmap) gains four themes beyond college data:

- **Accounts and households.** Sign in with email or Google and keep your lists and numbers. A parent can see each
  child's list and planning; a child never sees the parent's income or assets. Accounts are for ages 13 and up.
  ([accounts](../specs/product/accounts.md), [student profile](../specs/product/student-profile.md),
  [saved lists](../specs/product/saved-lists.md))
- **Planning tools.** Where your numbers stand at each college, with the reasons and never a made-up percentage;
  what your family would likely pay, as a range over four years; real aid offers side by side in one standard
  layout; and whether applying early helps. ([chances and fit](../specs/product/chances-and-fit.md),
  [net price estimator](../specs/product/net-price-estimator.md),
  [award letter analyzer](../specs/product/award-letter-analyzer.md),
  [early decision](../specs/product/early-decision-strategy.md))
- **High school context.** A page for every public high school and, where a counselor shares them, past
  applicants from your school drawn over each college's ranges.
  ([high school data](../specs/product/high-school-data.md), [scattergrams](../specs/product/scattergrams.md))
- **Platform, measurement, and business.** Usage measurement without cookies and without recording anyone's
  numbers; Free, Plus, and Pro tiers that keep every public number free; accounts for counselors; and a developer
  API. ([telemetry](../specs/product/telemetry.md), [commercialization](../specs/product/commercialization.md),
  [counselor portal](../specs/product/counselor-portal.md), [data API](../specs/product/data-api.md))

A [product overview](/roadmap/product) explains how the themes fit together, the build order, and the rules for
handling user data.

## Behind the scenes

The specs grew out of three idea documents (kept in `specs/product/ideas/`) and research into the 2026–27 federal
aid formula, the College Financing Plan and state award-letter laws, Scoir and Naviance exports, federal high
school datasets, analytics tools, and privacy law for minors. Nothing is built yet.
