## What's new

Nothing changes on the site. This writes down the settings behind it: where sign-in emails come from, how many can go
out an hour, where sign-in links may return, and the security record support can see.

## Behind the scenes

The settings live in our hosting providers' dashboards, not in the code, so they are now listed in the project's notes
with the step to repeat each one when the site moves to its production database.
