## What's new

- **Which colleges families are looking at.** The console's Colleges page opens with the ten most viewed colleges,
  for the last 7 days, the last 30 days, or all time, with how many signed-in people looked at each.

## Behind the scenes

It counts the same college-page views the console already shows per person and per college. Nothing new is recorded.
