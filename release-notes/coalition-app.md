## What's new

- **The other shared application.** The Coalition for College is the Common App's smaller rival: about 150 member
  colleges share one application, filled out through Scoir since 2023. Each member's admissions page now says, in
  the "What it takes to apply" card, when the college takes the Coalition application. Most of them take the Common
  App too, so it is usually a choice of form, not of colleges.
- **The advisor knows.** When you record how you applied, Coalition is offered only at a college that takes it, and
  the application card says "Takes the Common App and the Coalition application" before you pick.
- A glossary entry explains what the Coalition application is.

## Behind the scenes

Two public pages are read once a year through the polite crawler: the Coalition's member list (146 colleges on
October 28) and Scoir's list of colleges accepting applications through it (116, dated October 20). Each name is
joined to a college by its admissions link's domain, its name, or a hand-checked map, never a guess; every name
joined this time. The result is one block per member on the dataset, cited to the Coalition for College on every
value, with the source listed on the Data page.
