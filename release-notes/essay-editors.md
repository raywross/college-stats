## What's new

The essays roadmap gains a seventh part, editors. A parent or counselor will be able to edit their own copy of a
draft and send the changes as suggestions; the student sees the old words struck and the new ones underlined in that
person's color, and accepts or rejects each change, or all of them, with Word's three views: with changes, as it would
read, and the original. Nothing reaches the essay until the student accepts it.

The same part marks every reader's note, comment and open suggestion right in the draft, each author in their own
color, with a hover or tap that shows what is there and a jump to the details. The reader's corrected words (the one
kind of text Quad proposes) become one-tap changes.

## Behind the scenes

- The editor stays a plain text field; suggestions and marks are drawn around it, so phones, dictation and screen
  readers keep working as they do. Three decisions are the owner's and are listed at the end of the spec.
