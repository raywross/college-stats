## What's planned

Comparing colleges will start with a **short overview**: the colleges you picked, the biggest differences between
them, their shape on the radar, and one card per topic (getting in, students, academics, cost and aid, outcomes,
how they've changed) showing two or three figures side by side, with a bar per college. Tap a card for **that
topic's page** with everything the comparison shows today. The full table of every number stays, one tap away, with
a switch that hides rows where the colleges don't differ.

The map is the same one the college profiles use now, so moving from a profile to a comparison means nothing new to
learn. Read the plan, with the four layouts that were considered and how they scored, on the
[roadmap](/roadmap/compare-redesign).

## Behind the scenes

The review measured the compare page at three widths (about 10,000 pixels on a desktop and 15,000 on a phone, half
of it the 91-row table), applied the research on comparison tables, and scored the proposals with the same criteria
as the profile redesign. The topic pages reuse the profile's frame, pills, and header. Nothing is built yet.
