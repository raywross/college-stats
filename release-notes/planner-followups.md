## What's new

**Your weighted GPA shows as you entered it.** If your school adds points for honors, AP, or IB classes, a GPA like
4.4 now appears as "4.4 weighted" on your profile and at the top of your plan. Before, it was converted down and
shown as 4.0. The plan still compares colleges using the unweighted range your weighted GPA allows, so the groups
don't change.
