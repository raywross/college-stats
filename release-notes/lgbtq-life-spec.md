## What's planned

**[LGBTQ+ life](../specs/lgbtq-life.md)** joins religious life and Greek life under Campus life on the
[roadmap](/roadmap).

## What the research found

- **Federal counts exist but are sparse.** Since 2022–23, colleges can report students of "another gender". Across
  the site's 1,893 colleges, 206 report at least one student, 252 report 0, and 76% leave it blank. Nothing counts
  sexual orientation.
- **The standard rating is gone.** The Campus Pride Index is effectively offline. Its lists of trans-inclusive
  policies live on in the Trans Policy Clearinghouse.
- **Support changes fast.** Some campus centers have closed under new state laws, such as UT Austin's in 2024.

## Design choices

- No score, stars, or rank. Each fact stands alone with its source and "as of" date.
- Policies are checked on each college's own pages. Directory listings are leads to verify, not facts to publish.
- Conduct-code rules are quoted in the college's own words and reviewed by a person before publishing.
- Gender counts are never ranked or filtered, and blank ("not collected") is kept distinct from 0.
- Public colleges get a line on relevant state law, citing the statute.
