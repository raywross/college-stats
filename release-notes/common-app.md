## What's new

Common App publishes a Requirements Grid for its member colleges every cycle: which early rounds each college
offers and when they close, the application fee and whether the fee waiver is taken, whether a personal essay, a
writing supplement, or the Courses & Grades section is required, how many recommendations, and the test policy. With
Common App's permission, Quad now reads that grid and shows it on about a thousand college pages.

- **On a college's Admissions page**, a new card, **What it takes to apply**, lists the rounds offered with their
  dates ("Early Decision November 1 · ED II January 1 · Regular January 1", or "Rolling"), the fee and whether the
  fee waiver is accepted, the essay and supplement lines, the recommendations ("two teacher evaluations, a counselor
  recommendation, and a mid-year report"), and the test policy, including "flexible" where the college chooses among
  tests. Every line opens to its source and the cycle it describes. Colleges that don't take the Common App show no
  card.
- **Newest statement wins.** Where a college's own Common Data Set and the grid disagree on the regular deadline or
  the test policy, Quad shows the more recent statement and keeps the other one in the ⓘ. For this cycle that means
  the grid's test policy now stands at about a thousand colleges, and a few hundred colleges read differently than
  before: most often "required for some applicants" where the federal data said test-optional, or test-optional
  where it said test-blind. The test-optional trend pages follow the new values.
- **Explore** has an **Applying** group of filters: takes the fee waiver, offers Early Decision, offers ED II, offers
  Early Action (including Early Action II and Restrictive Early Action). "No application fee" now reads the grid's fee
  first. Only colleges with a grid row this cycle can match.
- **In the planner**, deadlines come from the grid first and the Common Data Set second, "Rolling" shows as a word,
  and each college's requirements checklist gains the essay, writing supplement, recommendations, Courses & Grades,
  fee waiver, and platform ("Takes the Common App") lines, each cited. Every member college's early rounds are known,
  not only those with a Common Data Set on file.
- **On Trends**, **This season on the Common App** shows how the application season went nationally months before
  federal data: applicants, applications per applicant, member count, and growth among fee-waiver-eligible and
  first-generation applicants, each cited to the End-of-Season report it comes from.
- **Search** knows more nicknames ("Vandy", "VU") from the colleges' Common App pages.

## What to know

The grid is Common App's statement of its members' requirements for the 2026-27 cycle, updated September 25, 2026;
colleges can change their rules mid-season, so the college's own page is the final word. Quad links only to
commonapp.org's homepage, never to a college's Common App page, and shows no Common App badge: the fact is the
deadline, not the vendor. International application fees are stored but not shown. The grid carries no
writing-supplement marks this cycle, so that line appears only where the grid shows one.
