## What's new

Nothing on the site changes. The four studies behind this week's visit calendars are now kept with the plan rather
than in a working folder: what 795 colleges' calendars contain by type of event, what the colleges on other systems
use instead, and how the two next-largest systems publish their events. Each is marked with what it measured and
what decision it led to, and the plan says where the build later corrected the research.

## Behind the scenes

They live in `specs/product/visit-events-research/`, linked from the visit-events plan and the product index. Local
file paths were removed; the raw pages they cite were working files and are not kept, apart from the fixtures the
tests use.
