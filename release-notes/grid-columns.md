## What's new

**Test policies corrected at 36 colleges.** A college's Common Data Set marks its test policy with an X under one of
five columns, and we had been recording some of those marks under the column next door. Each mark is now read from
where it sits on the page. Emory, the University of Chicago, Iowa State, Claremont McKenna, Boston College, and
Washington University in St. Louis, among others, now show as test-optional; North Carolina at Chapel Hill and
Villanova show "required for some applicants". Where a mark can't be placed with confidence, as at UT Austin and
Gettysburg, the page shows the federal figure until it can.

**Admission factors at 29 colleges.** How much a college weighs class rank, essays, recommendations, and the rest
follows the same check.

**The right year for four more documents.** Illinois Wesleyan's figures were filed under the wrong year, and UCLA's,
Calvin's, and Saint Vincent's newest Common Data Sets were held back because their covers' year couldn't be read. UCLA
and Calvin now show their 2025–26 retention and test score ranges.

## Behind the scenes
- 3,212 grid marks were placed by position (817 corrected); 958 that couldn't be placed safely are held.
- No college's selectivity tier changed.
