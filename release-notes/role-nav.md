## What's new

- **Students** see nothing different: Advisor is still one click away, and the avatar menu still has Household and
  Your account.
- **Parents** see **Household** where Advisor was. One click opens your child's advisor, as before; the small arrow
  beside it lists each child's advisor by name and **Manage household**, where you add someone or set your home
  address. Those used to hide in the avatar menu.
- **Counselors** see **Caseload**. One click opens your caseload; the arrow holds **Add students** and **Members and
  settings**. A counselor who is also a parent finds their own children there too.
- On a phone the middle tab changes the same way, and the More sheet lists that role's pages at the top.
- The avatar menu now holds only the personal things: Your account, Admin for an admin, Sign out.

## Behind the scenes

- The header's account answer carries a role (counselor if you belong to an organization; guardian if a student is on
  your household's roster or you signed up as a parent; student otherwise) and the names of your children.
- A parent's one click lands on the advisor rather than the roster because the advisor is the page a family opens
  daily; the roster is a weekly job.
- Opening your household or your caseload refreshes the header at once, so adding a child or starting an
  organization shows without waiting.
