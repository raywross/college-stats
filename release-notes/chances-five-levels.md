## What's planned

Nothing changes on the site with this release; these are plans, readable on the [roadmap](/roadmap) under
**Admission chances, next**.

- **Five levels instead of three.** Quad's estimate will read **High reach, Reach, Target, Likely, or Safe**. A
  student with top grades sees an Elite college as a Reach (a reach for everyone, and possible); a student far below
  its range sees a High reach. The list orders itself closest first, a High-reach Dream no longer starts in early
  decision, and the balance line asks for two colleges that are Likely or Safe.
- **Quad's selectivity.** Every college gets a tier, **Elite, Highly selective, Selective, Accessible, or Open**, from
  the figures it publishes: its admit rate for your state, its admitted students' scores and grades, yield, how much of
  the class it fills early, its ten-year direction, and how much it says it reads. Shown on the profile with the
  inputs listed.
- **Applicants like you.** "Applicants like you — from Indiana — were admitted at 71%" replaces "Your pool", with a
  panel listing every rate the college publishes for your situation.
- **Depth.** Your courses read as tracks by subject against your intended major, and an activities list read by
  years, role, and recognition in your top two, each with a suggestion, and counted only at colleges that say they
  read it.
- **Beyond the numbers.** Where you stand in your class, awards, work hours, a legacy toggle and a recruited-athlete
  pick per college, and interest you've shown, each used only where the college says it counts.

## Behind the scenes

The estimate's commitments are rewritten: it never uses race, ethnicity, sex, or religion; every individual in the
outcomes it learns from is anonymized; legacy is used only where a college weighs it and the state allows it; and
the student's high school isn't used in this version. The method's rules (never shown on the site) move to a third
version with a measured academic distance and the selectivity tier in place of a fixed 20% cutoff.
