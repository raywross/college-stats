## What's decided

About seventy-four colleges publish their visit calendars through TargetX, on sites that tell automated readers
not to read them. Quad built a reader for those calendars and then found this; the decision is to leave it
switched off. Those colleges, Texas Tech, the University of North Texas, Cal State Chico, Western Michigan, the
College of Charleston, and WPI among them, keep what every college has had all along: the link to their own visit
page.

What would change it: the colleges, or TargetX, offering the calendars another way, or a college's own site saying
readers are welcome, in which case that one college can be turned on by hand.

## Behind the scenes

The rule is the same one every page Quad reads has been told in its user agent: robots.txt is honoured. The
reasoning, the list, and the three options considered are in [the spec](../specs/product/visit-events.md), under
"TargetX: the protocol".
