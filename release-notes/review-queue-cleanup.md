## What's new

**2025–26 figures for eight more colleges.** Taylor, North Alabama, Bucknell, Troy, Cal Poly Humboldt, Farmingdale,
Binghamton, and Neumann now show retention, test score ranges, aid, and GPA profiles from their newest Common Data
Set. The official 2025–26 form still prints "2024–2025" at the top of every page, so our checks had filed these as
last year's and held them back. A few more colleges also gain newer graduating-class debt or student–faculty
ratios.

**Misread answers stay off.** In grids where a college marks a box under a column (how much it weighs class rank,
whether it requires test scores), we found marks recorded under the wrong column at seven of these colleges. Those
answers stay off their pages, and they keep showing the federal figures, until they're read again. That's why
Bucknell, Taylor, and Binghamton won't show a switch to "test required": their documents don't say that.

## Behind the scenes
- A check that compares a document's years with its edition now accepts either fall a college may quote (the
  entering cohort or the census), and the years inside a document outrank its page header.
- Re-checking stored documents now updates the list of figures waiting for review: about 6,000 entries that had
  already passed came off it.
- A person who finds a value misread can now hold it with a note of what the document shows; re-checks keep the
  hold.
- Planned: an assistant that works through the held figures by cause and proposes each fix with its evidence
  ([Data Steward](../specs/product/data-steward.md)).
